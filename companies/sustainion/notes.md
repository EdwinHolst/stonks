# Notes — Sustainion Group AB

## Data caveats

- **Exchange mismatch:** The user referenced "First North" but Sustainion is listed on **Spotlight Stock Market** (XSAT), a separate Swedish marketplace for smaller companies run by NGM. Ticker is SUSG; Yahoo Finance uses SUSG.ST.
- **Currency:** All financials in SEK. Single listing, so no multi-currency issue.
- **Fiscal year:** January 1 – December 31.
- **Acquisition-driven revenue:** Revenue more than doubled between FY 2024 (189 MSEK) and FY 2025 (538 MSEK) almost entirely because of two acquisitions — Skårebo Energi AB (closed Nov 2024, EV charging equipment wholesale) and Ströman Maskin AB (road/waste/winter machinery, closed 2025). Organic growth is structurally hard to read from the top line.
- **Share count stability:** 215.83M shares. Check for potential dilution from acquisition financing or directed issues — the company has used share issues in the past.
- **Thin margins:** Net margin has been 1–4% historically. Small absolute profit means a single bad quarter or integration hiccup can swing reported earnings significantly.
- **yfinance data quality:** Swedish small-caps on Spotlight often have incomplete or lagged data on yfinance/Yahoo. Cross-check against Spotlight filings or stockanalysis.com.
