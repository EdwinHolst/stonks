# LibertyStream Infrastructure Partners Inc — Overview

- **Ticker:** TSX Venture: LIB (also OTCQB: VLTLF, FSE: I2D)
- **Exchange:** TSX Venture Exchange (CVE/TSXV)
- **Sector / Industry:** Materials / Other Industrial Metals & Mining
- **HQ:** Calgary, Canada (re-domiciled to Texas, USA, effective 2026-04-17)
- **CEO:** James Wylie
- **Former name:** Volt Lithium Corp. (TSXV: VLT) — renamed June 2025

## Business model

Lithium development and technology company in North America. Core activity is
**direct lithium extraction (DLE)** from oilfield brines plus **lithium
carbonate refining**. Asset interests in the Permian Basin and Bakken Basin.
Has begun production from its DLE unit and lithium carbonate refining facility
at Select Water Solutions' site in Howard County (north-east of Midland, TX).

## Sector

Industrial metals & mining (lithium). Pre-/early-revenue, technology- and
project-execution-driven; tied to lithium pricing and DLE scale-up.
